Zo Schmux Recovery

A personal backup utility that helps its owner preserve and recover work from a cloud computer.

When authorized, it saves encrypted backup data in the owner's Google Drive account and reads that data to check or restore a backup. It uses Google Drive's drive.file permission for files created by this app or explicitly made available to it.

This page describes the utility. It does not provide access to the cloud computer, backups, conversations, or AI models.

Read the privacy policy.

Use of this tool

This utility is for its owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service. There is no public signup, subscription, or model access.

The owner controls authorization, backup retention, and deletion. The owner can disconnect the app through their Google Account permissions and remove its backup files from Drive.
