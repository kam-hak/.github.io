Zo Schmux Recovery

Privacy policy

Zo Schmux Recovery is a personal backup utility operated by the owner of this website for their own account. It is not a public service.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It can create and manage its own backup files, and access files explicitly made available to this app. It does not request access to the owner's entire Drive, Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

Purpose and storage

When authorized, the utility uploads encrypted backup data, reads backup metadata, checks stored data, and downloads backups for recovery. Backup contents are encrypted before upload. Google Drive stores the encrypted objects and their storage metadata.

The backup program runs on the owner's cloud computer. Authorization credentials and the encryption key are held in private storage available to the backup service. Restore operations decrypt data in private storage for the owner's use.

Sharing and use

The utility uses Google user data only to provide its personal backup and recovery functions. It does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models for training or analysis. It does not publish backup contents or share them with other users. Google Drive and the owner's hosting provider process the data as part of providing their services.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and can remove backup files from Drive. Disconnecting the app through Google Account permissions stops future authorized access. Disconnecting does not itself delete previously stored backup files.

Contact and changes

Contact the site owner through the main website with questions about this policy. This page will be updated if the utility's data practices change.
