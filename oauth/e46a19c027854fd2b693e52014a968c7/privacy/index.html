Privacy policy for zops-email-link

Personal application

zops-email-link is operated by Kamran Hakiman for personal use with his own Google accounts. This policy describes the Gmail assistant integration and automated notification sender associated with the application.

Data accessed

Depending on the Google permissions the owner grants and the functionality he requests, the application can access Google account identity information and Gmail data, including message contents, headers, and metadata. It also handles OAuth access and refresh credentials, and the contents and recipient addresses of automated maintenance and backup notices.

Gmail SMTP requires a broad mail authorization that can technically permit reading, sending, and deleting mail. The implemented notification sender sends configured notices. The separate assistant integration is configured to expose read-only Gmail tools.

How data is used

Google account data is used to provide email functionality requested by the owner: retrieving Gmail information through his configured assistant integration and sending automated maintenance and backup notices through Gmail SMTP.

The automated notification sender runs deterministic software. An AI agent is not needed to compose or transmit a standard notice.

Sharing and service providers

Requested Gmail information returned through the owner's assistant integrations may be processed by the assistant providers he has configured, under their respective terms and privacy policies. Google processes mailbox access and mail delivery. The owner's configured hosting and computing services run the integrations and may store their operational data.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. This integration does not use Google user data to train a general-purpose AI model. This statement describes the integration's use of data; the owner's selected assistant providers have their own data-handling terms.

Automated notices are delivered to the recipients configured by the owner. This application does not offer other people access to the owner's mailbox.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and configuration are stored on the owner's configured systems with access restricted to the operating account that needs them. Credentials are retained until replaced, revoked, or removed. They are not published on this website.

Mailbox messages remain subject to the owner's Google account settings. Local notification state and delivery records are retained as needed to operate and troubleshoot the notifications. Information returned to configured assistant services is subject to those services' retention settings and policies.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke this application's Google access through his Google Account connections page. Revoking access prevents future authorized operations but does not itself delete messages already delivered or records already stored.

To request removal of locally stored application credentials or records, or to ask a question about this policy, contact zops.kamran@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's handling of Google account data changes.
